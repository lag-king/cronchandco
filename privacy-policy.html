Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 3, 2026

Who we are

Cronch and Co. ("we", "us", "our") makes and sells small-batch chilli crisp and related food products. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you place an order through our order form or WhatsApp, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp number / phone number
  • Your email address, if you choose to provide one
  • Your delivery address, state, and pincode (for courier or Porter delivery orders)
  • Order details — items, quantities, and the amount payable

When you pay us online, payment is processed directly by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank account details on our own systems — Razorpay handles this in line with its own security standards and Privacy Policy.

How we use your information

  • To confirm, prepare, and fulfil your order
  • To contact you on WhatsApp, phone, or email about your order status, delivery, or payment
  • To arrange courier or Porter delivery to your address
  • To keep basic records of past orders for customer support purposes

We do not use your information for anything beyond running and fulfilling your order, and we do not send marketing messages unless you've asked us to.

Sharing of information

We share the minimum information necessary with:

  • Razorpay, to process your payment securely
  • Courier partners / Porter, to deliver your order to the address you provide

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or any other purpose.

Data storage & security

Order details you submit are used to process your order and are kept only as long as reasonably needed for order fulfilment, accounting, and customer support. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by writing to us using the contact details below. Providing your email address is always optional.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
